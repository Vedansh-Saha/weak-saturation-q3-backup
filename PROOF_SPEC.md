# Proof specification

## Main theorem

Establish
\[
\operatorname{wsat}(K_9,Q_3)=16,\qquad
\operatorname{wsat}(K_{10},Q_3)=18,\qquad
\operatorname{wsat}(K_{11},Q_3)=19.
\]

## Definitions and normalization

Let $C\cong Q_3$ be the canonical cube on vertices $0,\ldots,7$ and let $e^*=01$. Put
\[
U_n=E(K_n)\setminus E(C).
\]
If the first edge in a legal weak-saturation order is mapped to $01$, then $E(C)\setminus\{01\}$ is initially present.

For each labelled cube $Q\subseteq K_n$, define
\[
M(Q)=E(Q)\cap U_n.
\]
The outside closure is generated by all Horn implications
\[
M(Q)\setminus\{e\}\to e,
\qquad e\in M(Q).
\]
Duplicate masks give identical implications and may be merged.

The sequential equivalence and seed identity are
\[
\operatorname{cl}_n(S)=U_n
\iff
(C-e^*)\cup S \text{ saturates after } e^* \text{ is added},
\]
and
\[
\operatorname{wsat}(K_n,Q_3)=11+\sigma_n,
\qquad
\sigma_n=\min\{|S|:\operatorname{cl}_n(S)=U_n\}.
\]

## Closure algorithm

The implementation maintains the current set $T$ and, for each implication $A\to e$, the number of antecedent edges already in $T$. An edge entering $T$ is queued once. Whenever every edge of an antecedent $A$ has entered $T$, its target is inserted.

Correctness follows from two invariants:

1. Every inserted edge is justified by an implication whose antecedent is already contained in $T$; hence the computed set is contained in the mathematical closure.
2. At termination every implication with antecedent contained in $T$ has its target in $T$; hence $T$ is closed, so it contains the least closed superset of the seed.

At most $|U_n|$ edge insertions occur.

## Upper-bound certificates

For each certificate, every missing edge is supplied with an injective map
\[
\varphi_i:V(Q_3)\to V(K_n)
\]
with
\[
\varphi_i(0)\varphi_i(1)=e_i
\]
and
\[
\varphi_i(E(Q_3)\setminus\{01\})
\subseteq E(F_0)\cup\{e_1,\ldots,e_{i-1}\}.
\]
This is a finite certificate criterion: the image is a cube and exactly the target edge is required at step $i$.

The distributed witness tables give all maps and dependencies for $n=9,10,11$; `verify_witness_tables.py` checks injectivity, cube-image equality, target equality, initial-edge membership, dependency indices, and final completeness.

## n = 9

\[
|U_9|=24,
\qquad
\binom{24}{4}=10,626,
\qquad
\binom{24}{5}=42,504.
\]

The primary exact scan finds zero percolating four-edge seeds and maximum closure size 16. The same four-edge exclusion is obtained by the independent Go implementation using explicit edge lists and queue-indexed implications. The five-edge layer contains 6,864 percolating seeds.

The graph-level Python checker enumerates all
\[
840\binom98=7,560
\]
labelled copies of $Q_3$ in $K_9$ and verifies that the initial graph is $Q_3$-free and that the certificate is legal.

Therefore
\[
\sigma_9=5,
\qquad
\operatorname{wsat}(K_9,Q_3)=16.
\]

## n = 10

\[
|U_{10}|=33,
\qquad
\binom{33}{6}=1,107,568.
\]

The order-8 marked-cube stabilizer acts on $U_{10}$. Canonicalization yields 146,131 six-edge orbit representatives, none with full closure; the maximum closure has 25 outside edges.

The explicit 18-edge certificate gives the matching upper bound. The independent graph-level certificate check enumerates 37,800 labelled cube copies.

Hence
\[
\sigma_{10}=7,
\qquad
\operatorname{wsat}(K_{10},Q_3)=18.
\]

## n = 11

\[
|U_{11}|=43,
\qquad
\binom{43}{7}=32,224,114.
\]

The marked-cube stabilizer has order 24. The complete six-edge orbit layer contains 300,563 representatives. Adjoining one edge to every representative produces 11,120,831 children before deduplication and 1,498,208 seven-edge orbit representatives afterwards. No seven-edge representative has full closure; the maximum closure has 33 outside edges.

Burnside's lemma independently gives
\[
\frac{7,213,512}{24}=300,563,
\qquad
\frac{35,956,992}{24}=1,498,208.
\]

The explicit 19-edge certificate supplies the upper bound, so
\[
\sigma_{11}=8,
\qquad
\operatorname{wsat}(K_{11},Q_3)=19.
\]

## General extension

After completing a weakly saturated graph on $n$ old vertices to $K_n$, add a new vertex $x$ with two initial edges $xa,xb$. For any target $xy$, choose four further old vertices and embed $Q_3$ with $x$ replacing a cube vertex whose two other cube neighbours are $a$ and $b$. All cube edges except $xy$ are then present. Thus
\[
\operatorname{wsat}(K_{n+1},Q_3)\le\operatorname{wsat}(K_n,Q_3)+2,
\]
which with the $n=11$ certificate gives
\[
\operatorname{wsat}(K_n,Q_3)\le2n-3\qquad(n\ge11).
\]

## Analytic bounds

For $Q_3$ the Terekhov--Zhukovskii invariant gives
\[
e_i=2,4,6,7,9,10,11\qquad(1\le i\le7),
\]
and hence
\[
\min_i e_i/i=11/7.
\]
The corresponding asymptotic slope satisfies
\[
11/7\le c_{Q_3}\le2.
\]
The associated maximal subadditive lower-bound function gives the finite analytic bounds
\[
\operatorname{wsat}(K_9,Q_3)\ge13,
\quad
\operatorname{wsat}(K_{10},Q_3)\ge15,
\quad
\operatorname{wsat}(K_{11},Q_3)\ge17.
\]
The exact values 16, 18, and 19 require the finite exhaustive exclusions recorded above.
